# TALL ARK SERIES — Full-Swing Rotating Pull-Out

**Overview**
Full-height pull-out for tall cabinets. The whole basket glides out and rotates 360°, so every shelf is reachable from the front.

**Key features**
- Full-basket pull-out, 360° free rotation
- Soft-close runners for smooth, stable travel
- 2 / 4 / 5 / 6-tier and 6-tier tall options

**Cabinet fit**
Min. cabinet depth: 500 mm
Min. cabinet width: 600 mm
Min. cabinet opening: 564 × 500 mm (2-tier rotating models)
Basket depth: 475–477 mm
